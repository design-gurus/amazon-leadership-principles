# Amazon Leadership Principles: One-Page Cheat Sheet

Use this page for a final review. Each row gives the evidence an answer must contain and the most common question for that principle.

## The 16 principles

| Principle | Your answer must show | Most common question |
|-----------|-----------------------|----------------------|
| [Customer Obsession](../principles/customer-obsession.md) | A named customer and a decision made in their favor | A time you changed a plan because of customer feedback |
| [Ownership](../principles/ownership.md) | Action on a problem that was not assigned to you | A time you worked outside your responsibilities |
| [Invent and Simplify](../principles/invent-and-simplify.md) | Something you removed | A time you made something simpler |
| [Are Right, A Lot](../principles/are-right-a-lot.md) | Reasoning with incomplete information | A decision made without all the data |
| [Learn and Be Curious](../principles/learn-and-be-curious.md) | A skill learned because the work required it | Something you learned quickly |
| [Hire and Develop the Best](../principles/hire-and-develop-the-best.md) | Another person's measurable growth | Someone you mentored |
| [Insist on the Highest Standards](../principles/insist-on-the-highest-standards.md) | Work you refused to ship, and the cost | A time you were not satisfied with quality |
| [Think Big](../principles/think-big.md) | A proposal larger than the request | A bold idea you proposed |
| [Bias for Action](../principles/bias-for-action.md) | A fast, reversible decision with a safeguard | A time you acted without approval |
| [Frugality](../principles/frugality.md) | A result reached inside a real limit | A time you had limited resources |
| [Earn Trust](../principles/earn-trust.md) | A real mistake, admitted and repaired | A mistake you made |
| [Dive Deep](../principles/dive-deep.md) | Data you examined yourself | A time the data did not match the report |
| [Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit](../principles/have-backbone-disagree-and-commit.md) | Disagreement with a senior person, then full support | A time you disagreed with your manager |
| [Deliver Results](../principles/deliver-results.md) | A finished goal with a number | A goal you nearly missed |
| [Strive to be Earth's Best Employer](../principles/strive-to-be-earths-best-employer.md) | An improvement in how the team works | A time you improved team conditions |
| [Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility](../principles/success-and-scale-bring-broad-responsibility.md) | Care for people beyond the direct user | A time you considered a wider effect |

## The answer format

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, Result. Aim for two minutes in total.

| Part | Length | Content |
|------|--------|---------|
| Situation and Task | 30 seconds | The system, the scale, your responsibility, the constraint |
| Action | 70 seconds | Three to five steps, each beginning with "I" |
| Result | 20 seconds | One number, and what changed permanently |

## Facts about the loop

These facts are commonly reported and are not official.

- Four to six interviews, each 45 to 60 minutes.
- Each interviewer is assigned two or three principles.
- One interviewer is the Bar Raiser, who comes from outside the hiring team and can veto the hire.
- Each interviewer submits written feedback before the group discussion.

## Checklist for the day before

- [ ] 8 to 10 stories prepared, each mapped to at least two principles.
- [ ] One story about disagreement with a manager or a senior engineer.
- [ ] One story about a real mistake with a real cost.
- [ ] One story about finding the truth in the data.
- [ ] One story about working inside a hard limit.
- [ ] A number in every Result.
- [ ] Answers ready for "What would you do differently?" on every story.
- [ ] Three questions to ask each interviewer.
