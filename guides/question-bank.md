# Amazon Leadership Principles Question Bank

This page lists 96 behavioral questions, six for each of the 16 principles. Candidates commonly report questions of these forms, and the exact wording varies by interviewer. Each heading links to the page that explains what evidence the principle requires.

To practice, choose one question, answer it aloud in about two minutes using the [STAR method](star-method.md), and then answer the follow-up question "What would you do differently?"

## [Customer Obsession](../principles/customer-obsession.md)

1. Tell me about a time you changed a plan because of customer feedback.
2. Describe a time you went beyond what a customer asked for.
3. Tell me about a time you had to say no to a customer request.
4. Give an example of a time you worked backward from a customer need to a technical design.
5. Tell me about a difficult customer and how you handled the situation.
6. How do you find out what your customers need?

## [Ownership](../principles/ownership.md)

1. Tell me about a time you took on something outside your area of responsibility.
2. Describe a time you saw a problem that was not yours and fixed it.
3. Tell me about a time you made a short-term sacrifice for a long-term benefit.
4. Give an example of a project you started on your own initiative.
5. Tell me about a time a teammate's work was failing. What did you do?
6. Describe a time you continued working on a problem after others had stopped.

## [Invent and Simplify](../principles/invent-and-simplify.md)

1. Tell me about a time you made something simpler.
2. Describe an innovative solution you created for a problem.
3. Tell me about a time you removed a process or a feature.
4. Give an example of a time you solved a problem in a way nobody had tried before.
5. Tell me about a time your new idea was rejected. What happened next?
6. Describe a time you borrowed an idea from another team to solve your problem.

## [Are Right, A Lot](../principles/are-right-a-lot.md)

1. Tell me about a decision you made without all the data you wanted.
2. Describe a time you were wrong. How did you find out?
3. Tell me about a time you changed your mind because of new information.
4. Give an example of a judgment call that turned out well. How did you know it was correct?
5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with the data.
6. Describe a time you sought out an opinion that opposed yours.

## [Learn and Be Curious](../principles/learn-and-be-curious.md)

1. Tell me about something you had to learn quickly.
2. Describe a time you took on work outside your expertise.
3. Tell me about a time your curiosity led to an improvement.
4. What is the most useful thing you learned in the past year, and how did you apply it?
5. Tell me about a time you asked a question that changed a project's direction.
6. Describe a time you learned from a failure.

## [Hire and Develop the Best](../principles/hire-and-develop-the-best.md)

1. Tell me about someone you mentored. What can that person do now?
2. Describe a time you gave difficult feedback.
3. Tell me about the best hire you made, or the best candidate you interviewed.
4. Tell me about a time you decided not to hire a candidate whom others liked.
5. How have you helped a low performer improve?
6. Describe a time you gave a teammate an assignment beyond their current skills.

## [Insist on the Highest Standards](../principles/insist-on-the-highest-standards.md)

1. Tell me about a time you were not satisfied with the quality of something.
2. Describe a time you refused to release work that others considered ready.
3. Tell me about a time you raised the quality standard on your team.
4. Give an example of a time you found a defect that others had missed.
5. Tell me about a time you could not meet your own standard. What did you do?
6. How do you balance quality against a deadline? Give a specific example.

## [Think Big](../principles/think-big.md)

1. Tell me about a time you proposed a bold idea.
2. Describe a time you looked beyond the immediate task and saw a larger opportunity.
3. Tell me about your most ambitious project.
4. Give an example of a time you set a goal that others thought was too high.
5. Tell me about a time you convinced others to adopt a long-term vision.
6. Describe a time a narrow solution would have worked, and you chose a wider one. Why?

## [Bias for Action](../principles/bias-for-action.md)

1. Tell me about a time you acted without waiting for approval.
2. Describe a decision you made quickly with incomplete information.
3. Tell me about a calculated risk you took.
4. Give an example of a time you removed an obstacle that was slowing your team.
5. Tell me about a time you acted too quickly. What did you learn?
6. Describe a time you had to choose between more analysis and a deadline.

## [Frugality](../principles/frugality.md)

1. Tell me about a time you delivered with limited resources.
2. Describe a time you reduced a cost.
3. Tell me about a time you had to do more with less.
4. Give an example of a time you chose not to spend money that was available.
5. Tell me about a time you found a cheaper way to reach the same result.
6. Describe a time a budget limit forced you to change your design.

## [Earn Trust](../principles/earn-trust.md)

1. Tell me about a mistake you made.
2. Describe a time you received critical feedback. What did you do with it?
3. Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news.
4. Tell me about a time you had to rebuild a working relationship.
5. Give an example of a time you disagreed with a colleague and kept their trust.
6. Tell me about a time you missed a commitment.

## [Dive Deep](../principles/dive-deep.md)

1. Tell me about a time the data did not match what you were told.
2. Describe the most difficult problem you have debugged.
3. Tell me about a time you found the root cause of a recurring problem.
4. Give an example of a time you questioned a metric.
5. Tell me about a time you had to understand an unfamiliar system in detail.
6. Describe a time when looking at the details changed a decision.

## [Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit](../principles/have-backbone-disagree-and-commit.md)

1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
2. Describe a time you challenged a decision made by someone senior.
3. Tell me about a time you committed to a decision you did not agree with.
4. Tell me about an unpopular position you defended.
5. Give an example of a time you were overruled. What did you do next?
6. Describe a time you spoke up when it was uncomfortable to do so.

## [Deliver Results](../principles/deliver-results.md)

1. Tell me about a goal you nearly missed. How did you recover?
2. Describe your most significant professional achievement.
3. Tell me about a time you delivered a project under a tight deadline.
4. Give an example of a time you had too many priorities. How did you choose?
5. Tell me about a time you did not meet a goal.
6. Describe a time you had to overcome a major obstacle to finish a project.

## [Strive to be Earth's Best Employer](../principles/strive-to-be-earths-best-employer.md)

1. Tell me about a time you improved your team's working conditions.
2. Describe a time you supported a colleague who was struggling.
3. Tell me about a time you made your team more inclusive.
4. How have you helped a teammate grow in their career?
5. Tell me about a time you noticed that a teammate was overloaded. What did you do?
6. Describe a time you changed a process to make your team's work more sustainable.

## [Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility](../principles/success-and-scale-bring-broad-responsibility.md)

1. Tell me about a time you considered a wider effect of your work.
2. Describe a time you raised an ethical or privacy concern.
3. Tell me about a decision in which you weighed the customer's request against a larger responsibility.
4. Give an example of a time you made a product more accessible.
5. Tell me about a time you did the right thing when nobody was checking.
6. Describe a time you reduced an unintended harmful effect of a system.
