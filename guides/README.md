# Guides

These pages cover the parts of Amazon behavioral preparation that are not specific to one principle: how the interview day is run, how to structure an answer, and how to prepare stories.

| Page | Read it when |
|------|--------------|
| [How the Amazon loop works](how-the-amazon-loop-works.md) | You want to know who the interviewers are and how the decision is made |
| [The STAR method](star-method.md) | You need a structure for your answers |
| [Building a story bank](story-bank.md) | You are deciding which stories to prepare |
| [Question bank](question-bank.md) | You want questions to practice with |
| [Principles by role](principles-by-role.md) | You want to know which principles matter most for your position |
| [Common mistakes](common-mistakes.md) | You have draft answers and want to check them |
| [One-page cheat sheet](one-page-cheat-sheet.md) | You need a final review before the interview |

A reasonable order for a first reading is the loop, the STAR method, and the story bank, followed by the [16 principle pages](../principles/README.md).
