# Hire and Develop the Best

Hire and Develop the Best means raising the level of the team with every hire and helping other people grow. A candidate who has never managed anyone can still answer with mentoring, interviewing, or onboarding, as long as the story shows what the other person could do afterwards.

## What the interviewer listens for

- A named person or a specific hire, described with respect.
- What the candidate did: regular feedback, a harder assignment, or a defended hiring decision.
- Evidence of growth, such as a promotion, an independent project, or a measurable change in output.
- For managers: a hiring standard that was held even when the position was urgent.

## Questions

1. Tell me about someone you mentored. What can that person do now?
2. Describe a time you gave difficult feedback.
3. Tell me about the best hire you made, or the best candidate you interviewed.
4. Tell me about a time you decided not to hire a candidate whom others liked.
5. How have you helped a low performer improve?
6. Describe a time you gave a teammate an assignment beyond their current skills.

## Weak and strong answers

| Answer | What it contains |
|--------|------------------|
| Weak | General statements about enjoying mentoring, or advice given once with no knowledge of what happened next |
| Strong | The person's starting point, the specific actions over weeks or months, and proof of what the person achieved later |

## Sample answer

> "A new graduate joined my team and, after three months, her code reviews still needed four or five rounds. I asked my manager if I could mentor her. We met for 30 minutes twice a week. Instead of correcting her code, I asked her to review mine first and explain every comment. After six weeks, her reviews were passing in one or two rounds. I then proposed that she own the migration of our notification templates, a project touching 40 files. She finished it in five weeks with no production defects. A year later she was promoted, and she now mentors our interns."
