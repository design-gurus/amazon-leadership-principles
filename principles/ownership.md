# Ownership

Ownership means acting on a problem because it affects the product or the company, even when the problem belongs to nobody or to another team. It also means thinking about the long-term effect of a decision rather than only the current deadline.

## What the interviewer listens for

- A problem that was outside the candidate's assigned work.
- The moment the candidate decided to act, and why nobody else was going to.
- Follow-through until the problem was closed, including the unpleasant final steps.
- A change that prevents the problem from returning, such as an alert, a test, or a document.

## Questions

1. Tell me about a time you took on something outside your area of responsibility.
2. Describe a time you saw a problem that was not yours and fixed it.
3. Tell me about a time you made a short-term sacrifice for a long-term benefit.
4. Give an example of a project you started on your own initiative.
5. Tell me about a time a teammate's work was failing. What did you do?
6. Describe a time you continued working on a problem after others had stopped.

## Weak and strong answers

| Answer | What it contains |
|--------|------------------|
| Weak | A task that was already assigned to the candidate, or a problem that was reported to someone else and then left |
| Strong | A clear statement that the work was not assigned, the action taken, coordination with the real owner, and a permanent fix |

## Sample answer

> "During a release week I noticed that our nightly database backup job had been reporting success with a file size of zero for 11 days. The job belonged to the infrastructure team, and their on-call engineer was handling an outage. I told them what I had found and offered to investigate. A storage credential had expired, and the script did not check the upload result. I renewed the credential with their approval, ran a manual backup, and tested a restore. I then added a size check and an alert, and wrote a one-page summary for both teams. The backup has not failed silently in the eight months since."
