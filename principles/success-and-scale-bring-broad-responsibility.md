# Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility

Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility means considering the effect of one's work on people beyond the direct user, including communities, partners, and the environment. It is one of the two principles that Amazon added later than the first 14, and a dedicated story is rarely necessary.

## What the interviewer listens for

- Awareness of a secondary effect: privacy, accessibility, security, energy use, or harm to a third party.
- A decision in which the candidate accepted extra work to reduce that effect.
- Evidence that the candidate raised the concern before anyone required it.
- A concrete result, even a small one.

## Questions

1. Tell me about a time you considered a wider effect of your work.
2. Describe a time you raised an ethical or privacy concern.
3. Tell me about a decision in which you weighed the customer's request against a larger responsibility.
4. Give an example of a time you made a product more accessible.
5. Tell me about a time you did the right thing when nobody was checking.
6. Describe a time you reduced an unintended harmful effect of a system.

## Weak and strong answers

| Answer | What it contains |
|--------|------------------|
| Weak | Volunteer work unrelated to the job, or a compliance task that was simply assigned |
| Strong | A secondary effect that the candidate noticed without being asked, the extra cost accepted, and the outcome for the affected people |

## Sample answer

> "I was building a feature that suggested contacts to new users of a messaging product. The fastest design uploaded each user's full address book to our servers. During design review, I pointed out that this would store the phone numbers of people who had never agreed to our terms, about 200 contacts per user. Nobody had asked for a privacy review, so I requested one myself. I proposed hashing the numbers on the device, which converts each number into a fixed code so that the raw numbers never leave the phone, and deleting unmatched codes within 24 hours. The change added nine days to the schedule. The privacy team later adopted the design as the standard for two other products."
