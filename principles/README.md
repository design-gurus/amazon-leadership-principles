# The 16 Amazon Leadership Principles

Each page below covers one principle in the same format: its meaning in plain words, the evidence an interviewer listens for, the questions asked, the difference between a weak and a strong answer, and one sample answer.

The official wording of every principle is on [Amazon's Leadership Principles page](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles). The pages here paraphrase it for interview preparation.

| Principle | The evidence an answer must contain |
|-----------|-------------------------------------|
| [Customer Obsession](customer-obsession.md) | You started from the customer's problem, and you know who the customer was |
| [Ownership](ownership.md) | You acted beyond your assignment and stayed until the problem was closed |
| [Invent and Simplify](invent-and-simplify.md) | You removed something, not only built something |
| [Are Right, A Lot](are-right-a-lot.md) | Your judgment held with incomplete information, and you can explain why |
| [Learn and Be Curious](learn-and-be-curious.md) | You learned something new because the work required it |
| [Hire and Develop the Best](hire-and-develop-the-best.md) | Another person became better, with proof of what they did afterwards |
| [Insist on the Highest Standards](insist-on-the-highest-standards.md) | You refused to ship something, and you state what that cost |
| [Think Big](think-big.md) | You proposed something larger than the request, with reasoning |
| [Bias for Action](bias-for-action.md) | You moved without complete information on a reversible decision |
| [Frugality](frugality.md) | You reached the result inside a real limit |
| [Earn Trust](earn-trust.md) | You were honest about a mistake or accepted criticism |
| [Dive Deep](dive-deep.md) | You examined the data yourself and found what the summary missed |
| [Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit](have-backbone-disagree-and-commit.md) | You disagreed with a senior person, then supported the decision |
| [Deliver Results](deliver-results.md) | You finished, and the result is a number |
| [Strive to be Earth's Best Employer](strive-to-be-earths-best-employer.md) | You improved how the team works |
| [Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility](success-and-scale-bring-broad-responsibility.md) | You considered people beyond the direct user |
